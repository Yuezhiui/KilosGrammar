### Ready scene — known user-directed removal, not equivalent to a dead test fixture

At the **initial** snapshot, `ui/play/ready/ready.tscn` and `ready.gd` existed as a dynamically registered but effectively bypassed route: `main_menu.gd` converted Ready to Calibration or Gameplay before loading a panel. However, **three QA fixtures loaded the Ready scene directly**: `tools/test_guided_routing.gd`, `tools/test_shutdown_resources.gd`, and `tools/test_ui_feature_layout_20261029.gd`. Bare removal at that point would have broken them.

**During this read-only audit, another owner removed `ui/play/ready/` and updated the checked router/fixture references.** A final filesystem check confirmed `ready.tscn` and its directory no longer exist, and no `ready.tscn`/`ready.gd` reference remained under current `ui/` and `tools/` GDScript searches. Therefore it is **already removed by another agent**, not a new deletion recommendation from this report. Historical docs and migration reports can be stale; do not reintroduce Ready because an older document lists it.
