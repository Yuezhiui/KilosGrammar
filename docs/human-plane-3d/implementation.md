# Human Plane 3D implementation — 26 October 2026

Human Plane now **renders a real 3D flying world** while retaining the previous `Control`-based scoring, teacher setup, webcam pose tracking, shared HUD and game-navigation APIs.

## Scene architecture

`ui/minigames/human_plane.tscn` now instances `WorldView3D` (z-index 3) between the original 2D collision/layout guides and the HUD `CanvasLayer`. The old 2D plane and rectangular gate sprites have alpha 0 at runtime and cannot render over the 3D view; they are kept as geometry for the existing calibrated collision and test contracts, not as gameplay visuals. `ui/minigames/human_plane_3d_view.tscn` contains the `SubViewportContainer`, a real `SubViewport`, `WorldEnvironment`, `Camera3D`, directional light, grassy ground, flight path, trees, rocks, and a glTF toy airplane. `human_plane_3d_view.gd` translates body X into player X and recycles trees/rocks with `delta`-based movement, suspending movement during Pause.

`ui/minigames/human_plane_3d_ring.tscn` is a reusable 3D torus gate: an editable native `TorusMesh`, highlight mesh, centered `Label3D` using the existing Titan One font, and lane-specific color/material. Three current and three prefetched torus gates show the **actual words** generated by the GrammarBank. A prompt such as “WHICH WORD IS A NOUN?” retains its normal grammar meaning; the rings read `ASTRONOMER`, `IMAGINATIVE`, `CATCHERS`, etc., not hardcoded category labels. This keeps the original correct/incorrect scoring sound.

**Movement and scenery optimization:** The airplane advances visually by keeping a nearly stationary forward-Z position and smoothly following the calibrated student body horizontally. Current and prefetched torus rings move toward the plane on the same shared Z curve, preventing a jump on question promotion; a bounded 25 ms render-time predictor interpolates between gameplay samples without altering authoritative hit detection. Rings are **blue / green / orange** in left/center/right order. Four reusable forest strips contain **120 low-poly trees and 760 opaque grass tufts** drawn through **12 GPU MultiMesh nodes** instead of nearly 900 independent scene nodes, supplemented by fixed procedural mountains and the user-supplied tinted NATURE FBX scenery. Their nodes are pooled/recycled; no per-frame instantiation. The 2K sky derivative removes most panoramic texture bandwidth while keeping the original 6K source. Directional shadows and distant visibility ranges were lowered. The motion/forest/GPU tests and more detailed measurements are in `qa.md` and `performance.md`.

**Updated scoring (26 October):** Seconds = 60s, Lives = three hearts, correct = **+100**, wrong gate = **0 point deduction**, missed gates = **0 point deduction**. In Lives mode, a wrong/missed gate still costs one heart. Difficulty and no-repeat vocabulary remain unchanged. The 3D ring Z position is derived from the original `gate_progress` and exact answer-line crossing fraction; 3D presentation never awards points independently. The airplane's calibrated left/right movement is still locked to **both arms spread and fresh tracking**. Keyboard movement is available only with the exported `allow_keyboard_debug = true`, **disabled** in normal play.

**Recorded voice-over:** `human_plane.gd` reuses the real positive/negative/miss MP3 clips from `res://assets/audio/catch_voice/` (same as Catch the Word). One persistent `AudioStreamPlayer` on `KilosSFX`, a small bounded clip cache loaded before the first collision, and explicit pause/scene-exit stop prevent duplicate players or per-frame audio loading. Headless QA checks the chosen streams without starting a dummy output device.

The NATURE asset originals are copied into `Components/Gameplay/Human Plane/3D/`. No absolute Downloads paths are loaded by the game; the four scenery FBX assets and glTF/textures are real imported resources. Their license/attribution obligations are in that directory's README. The provided public [3D endless runner](https://github.com/mujtaba-io/3d-endless-runner) served as a reference for the lane/world-scrolling concept; no upstream game code was imported.

## QA

Core tests: `tools/test_human_plane_3d_world.gd` (camera, torus meshes and words at 3 resolutions), `tools/test_human_plane_3d_interactions.gd` (correct/incorrect scoring, pose hold/freeze, pause and opt-in keyboard), `tools/test_human_plane_3d_visual.gd` (real GPU screenshots at 1280×720, 1600×900 and 1920×1080, near/distant), plus original `test_human_plane_fixed_score_speed.gd`, `test_human_plane_body_steering.gd`, `test_human_plane_body_integration.gd`, and minigame/global asset tests. All tests avoid real database writes. Offline render checks do **not** establish camera+YOLO end-to-end FPS or school-room lighting accuracy: verify with the actual camera and RTX/integrated GPU before classroom deployment.
